# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Notes on satellite and aerial imagery, Earth observation data, and the tools and models used to analyze them.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Wherobots) compares Earth [[Embeddings]] products (Clay, Major TOM, AlphaEarth, Tessera, Presto, Copernicus-Embed, Earth Index). He argues that mismatched grids, CRS choices and file formats make them costly to use, and calls for shared cloud-native standards, benchmarks and provenance. *Keywords: earth embeddings, foundation models, cloud-native, interoperability, technical debt*
- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Python toolkit for delineating field boundaries from satellite imagery. Main topic: [[Agriculture]].
