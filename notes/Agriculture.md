# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural applications of geospatial data, such as crop mapping and field boundary delineation.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Open-source [[Python]] toolkit for delineating agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Landsat, Sentinel-2, HLS, NAIP, SPOT). It runs [[Deep_Learning]] engines such as Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, Mask R-CNN, DINOv3 and Prithvi, plus [[Embeddings]] clustering, with optional SAM 2 refinement. *Keywords: field boundaries, segmentation, satellite imagery, Google Earth Engine, foundation models*
